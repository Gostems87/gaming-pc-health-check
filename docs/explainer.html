Ground rules it never breaks

  • Nothing changes without your yes, and every setting it touches goes in an undo file first.
  • It never touches your BIOS. You get a printed-style card with what normal looks like and when to stop.
  • Your games, clips and files are yours. Nothing is deleted without asking about each one.
  • No snake oil. No "FPS boost" registry packs, no overclocking past your memory's own factory profile.
  • Results stay on your PC, in a dated folder with the full report.
